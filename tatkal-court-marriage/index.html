Frequently Asked Questions (Tatkal Court Marriage)

How quickly can we get married in Guwahati?

Under the Special Marriage Act 1954, there is a statutory 30-day notice period. However, if a traditional marriage is solemnized, registration under the Hindu Marriage Act can be processed on an urgent basis.

Can outstation / non-resident couples register in Guwahati?

Yes. At least one partner must reside within Kamrup Metropolitan / Guwahati district for a minimum of 30 days prior to notice filing, backed by local address proof or legal affidavit.
