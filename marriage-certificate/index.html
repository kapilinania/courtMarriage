How to Get a Legal Marriage Certificate in Guwahati, Assam

A Government Marriage Certificate is an essential legal document that proves the marital status of a couple. In Assam, marriage certificates are issued by the Marriage Registrar Office under either the Special Marriage Act, 1954 or the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 via the government RTPS e-District portal.

Whether you need to register a newly solemnized court marriage or regularize an existing traditional marriage, Advocate Khusboo Verma ensures seamless filing, document verification, and timely certificate issuance.

Uses & Importance of Official Marriage Certificate

  • Spouse Visa & Immigration: Mandatory for Canada, US, UK, Europe, Australia spouse visa applications.
  • Passport Name Change / Addition: Required by Regional Passport Office (RPO) Guwahati to add spouse name.
  • Banking & Joint Property: Essential for opening joint bank accounts, home loans, and insurance claim settlements.
  • Legal Security & Inheritance: Acts as primary legal proof in all judicial courts across India.

Marriage Certificate FAQs

Can we correct errors in an existing marriage certificate?

Yes, spelling errors or date mismatches can be rectified through formal application and legal affidavit before the Marriage Registrar in Guwahati.

How do I verify my RTPS Assam Marriage Certificate?

You can scan the QR code printed on the official certificate or enter the application reference number on the Govt. of Assam RTPS portal.
