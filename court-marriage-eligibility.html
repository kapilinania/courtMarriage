Eligibility for Court Marriage in Guwahati

Essential legal conditions prescribed under the Special Marriage Act, 1954 for marriage registration approval.

Before applying for a court marriage in Guwahati, both the bride and bridegroom must fulfil the eligibility conditions prescribed under the Special Marriage Act, 1954. Meeting these legal requirements is essential for the approval of your marriage application by the Marriage Officer.

1. Minimum Age for Court Marriage

To register a court marriage in Guwahati:

  • The bridegroom must be at least 21 years old.
  • The bride must be at least 18 years old.

Valid age proof such as a Birth Certificate, Class X Admit Card, Passport, or any government-issued document must be submitted.

2. Both Parties Must Be Unmarried

Neither party should have a living spouse at the time of the marriage. If either party is divorced or widowed, the relevant legal documents, such as a Divorce Decree or Death Certificate of the previous spouse, must be produced.

3. Free and Voluntary Consent

Both parties must willingly agree to marry without any force, fraud, coercion, or undue influence. The Marriage Officer must be satisfied that the consent has been given freely.

4. Mental Capacity

Both the bride and bridegroom must be mentally capable of understanding the nature of marriage and giving valid consent. They should not suffer from any mental condition that makes them legally incapable of entering into a marriage.

5. Not Within Prohibited Degrees of Relationship

The parties should not be related to each other within the prohibited degrees of relationship as specified under the Special Marriage Act, unless such a marriage is permitted by their applicable customs or personal law.

6. Residence Requirement for Court Marriage in Guwahati

One of the parties must have been ordinarily residing within the jurisdiction of the concerned Marriage Officer in Guwahati for at least 30 days immediately before submitting the Notice of Intended Marriage. A valid residence proof of Guwahati is required to establish this requirement.

Who Can Apply for Court Marriage in Guwahati?

The Special Marriage Act allows the following couples to legally marry in Guwahati:

Interfaith Marriages:

Couples belonging to different religions marrying under civil law without conversion.

Inter-Caste Marriages:

Couples belonging to different castes desiring quick, 100% legal court registration.

Same Religion Civil Marriage:

Couples preferring a quiet, hassle-free legal registration instead of traditional ceremonies.

NRIs & Foreign Nationals:

Indian citizens & foreign nationals satisfying jurisdictional requirements.
