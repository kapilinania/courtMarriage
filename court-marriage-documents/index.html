Documents Required for Court Marriage in Guwahati

Comprehensive list of mandatory documents to present with your court marriage application to the Marriage Registrar in Guwahati.

Following is the official list of documents to be presented with the court marriage application to the Marriage Registrar in Guwahati for court marriage in Guwahati by the couple under the Special Marriage Act, 1954:

1. Non-Prohibited Blood Relationship Declaration

Mandatory 01

An official affidavit and declaration confirming that the bride and bridegroom are not within the prohibited degrees of relationship as defined under the Special Marriage Act, 1954 (unless permitted by established custom).

Download Declaration Form (PDF) View All Forms

2. Signed Intended Marriage Notice

Mandatory 02

A formal copy of the Notice of Intended Marriage duly signed by both parties (bride & groom), prepared according to the Second Schedule of the Special Marriage Act and submitted to the Marriage Officer in Guwahati.

Download Notice Form (PDF) View All Forms

3. Proof of Residence (Bride & Groom)

Mandatory 03

Valid proof of residence establishing that at least one of the parties has been ordinarily residing in Guwahati for at least 30 days prior to notice filing. Acceptable documents include:

  • Passport (Current Address)
  • Voter ID Card (Election Commission)
  • Driving Licence
  • Aadhaar Card / Utility Bill / Bank Passbook (Guwahati jurisdiction)
View All Forms

4. Proof of Age (Bride 18+ & Groom 21+)

Mandatory 04

Official government document verifying legal age eligibility (Male ≥ 21 Years, Female ≥ 18 Years):

  • High School (H.S.LC / Class X) Admit Card or Pass Certificate
  • Official Birth Certificate issued by Municipal Corporation / Authority
  • Valid Indian Passport
View All Forms

5. Documents for Divorced / Widowed Spouse

Special Case 05

If either party was previously married, valid legal proof of dissolution of previous marriage must be produced:

a. For Divorced Spouse: Certified copy of final Divorce Decree issued by Family Court / District Court.
b. For Widowed Spouse: Official Death Certificate of the deceased spouse.

View All Forms

6. Documents Required if Party is Foreign National

Special Case 06

For court marriage in Guwahati involving an NRI or Foreign National spouse:

a. Single Status Certificate / No Objection Certificate (NOC): Issued by the Embassy / High Commission / Consulate of their respective Country.
b. Valid Passport & Visa / OCI Card: Proof of legal stay in India.

View All Forms

7. Post-Social Marriage Registration Documents

Optional 07

If the couple is registering their marriage after a social / religious wedding ceremony (under personal laws or Section 16 of Special Marriage Act):

• Marriage invitation card, temple/religious certificate, or photographs of the wedding ceremony establishing social marriage.
