Timeline Overview

The 7-Step Court Marriage Procedure

A structured walkthrough of the legal procedure under the Special Marriage Act, 1954.

01

Legal Consultation

Contact Advocate Khusboo Verma to check age limits, local residency proof, and select the correct marriage act.

02

Document Verification

Verification of ID cards, age proofs, spelling issues, witness list, and photos. We resolve documentation gaps.

03

Filing Application

Our legal team drafts affidavits, fills out official application forms, and submits the notice to the Marriage Officer.

04

Notice Period

A mandatory 30-day notice is displayed by the Registrar. Objections on legal eligibility can be raised during this time.

05

Court Appearance

Both parties and 3 adult witnesses appear physically at the office of the Marriage Registrar with original identity proofs.

06

Marriage Registration

Declarations are signed by the couple and witnesses. The Marriage Officer countersigns and registers the entries.

07

Certificate Issuance

An official, legally binding Government Court Marriage Certificate is generated and handed over to the couple.

Legal Warnings

Important Procedure Guidelines

Critical instructions couples must comply with to ensure standard legal execution and zero delays.

Strict Age Limits

Applications with age discrepancies (under 18 for girls, 21 for boys) cannot be accepted under any circumstances. Providing forged birth records is a serious legal offense.

Physical Witness Presence

All 3 witnesses must physically appear at the registration office. Remote signatures, online video call confirmations, or proxy signings are not allowed by law.

Objections & Notice Displays

Under the Special Marriage Act, a notice of intention to marry is displayed for 30 days. Objections regarding pre-existing marriages can halt solemnization.
