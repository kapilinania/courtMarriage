Check Eligibility

Court Marriage Eligibility Criteria

Before proceeding, ensure both parties meet these statutory requirements under Indian law.

Groom (Boy) Requirements

  • Age must be 21 years or older
  • Must give free and voluntary consent
  • Must be of sound mental health
  • Status: Unmarried, Legally Divorced, or Widower
  • Must not fall under prohibited relationships

Bride (Girl) Requirements

  • Age must be 18 years or older
  • Must give free and voluntary consent
  • Must be of sound mental health
  • Status: Unmarried, Legally Divorced, or Widow
  • Must not fall under prohibited relationships
Document Checklist

Required Documents for Court Marriage

Keep these essential documents ready to ensure a swift, error-free application process.

Bride & Groom Documents

  • Age Proof

    Birth Certificate, Matriculation (10th) Certificate, or Passport.

  • Identity Proof

    Aadhar Card, PAN Card, or Voter ID.

  • Address Proof

    Electricity Bill, Rent Agreement, Voter ID, or Passport.

  • Passport Size Photos

    6 recent color photographs of both the Bride and Groom.

  • Affidavit

    Affidavit detailing age, marital status, and consent (we will draft this).

Witness & Special Case Documents

  • Three Witnesses

    Any 3 adults (friends, relatives, neighbors) with valid ID and Address proof.

  • Witness ID Proof

    Aadhar Card, PAN Card, Passport, or Driving License of all witnesses.

  • Witness Photos

    2 passport-size photographs of each witness.

  • Divorce / Death Certificate (If Applicable)

    Certified copy of Divorce Decree if divorced, or Spouse's Death Certificate if widowed.
