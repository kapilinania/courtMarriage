Under Indian law, the statutory minimum age for marriage is 21 years for the groom (boy) and 18 years for the bride (girl). Applications with ages below these limits cannot be accepted.

Under the Special Marriage Act, there is a mandatory 30-day notice/objection period, so the process takes about 30 to 35 days. However, under the Hindu Marriage Act (for marriages already performed traditionally), the registration and certificate can be processed much faster (typically within a few days).
