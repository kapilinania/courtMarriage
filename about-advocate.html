Legal Counselor Profile

Advocate Sunita Baruah, B.A. LL.B

Advocate Sunita Baruah is a highly respected Matrimonial and Family Law specialist practicing at the Gauhati High Court and local district family courts. With over 15 years of dedicated practice, she has successfully guided more than 1200 couples through legal court marriages, Compulsory Marriage Registrations, and Arya Samaj filings.

She believes in making legal procedures smooth, affordable, and accessible. Her chamber specializes in drafting legally robust affidavits, verifying witnesses, and providing security consultations to inter-caste and inter-faith couples under secular Indian laws.
